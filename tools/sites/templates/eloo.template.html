05 — What it means

Four things the map is telling us

FINDING 01

Flipkart Minutes is a whitespace

Flipkart Minutes delivers in only 9 Bengaluru pincodes, and in every one of them it stocks only Jivo canola — zero olive oil of any grade. Listing Extra Light here is pure upside, not a defence play.

FINDING 02

A real distribution gap, not a data gap

Blinkit delivers to ~88 Bengaluru pincodes but stocks Extra Light in 78, and 5 more list it only as out-of-stock. The handful of serve-but-no-Jivo pockets (e.g. 560010, 560108) are concrete placement targets.

FINDING 03

The core is Blinkit ∩ Zepto

64 pincodes carry it on both platforms — resilient coverage. But 26 rest on a single platform; if that one delists, the pincode goes dark. Worth a redundancy push.

FINDING 04

27 pincodes are dark

27 of 117 Bengaluru pincodes can’t get Extra Light from any of the three — mostly outer pincodes none of the platforms deliver to yet (560007, 560024, the 562xxx fringe). These move only when q-commerce serviceability expands.

06 — Methodology & provenance

How this was measured

Question
Of all Bengaluru pincodes, how many have Jivo Extra Light Olive Oil present across Blinkit, Zepto & Flipkart Minutes.
SKU definition
Any canonical SKU matching extra-light-olive-oil (all pack sizes: 1L, 2L, combo 2L/4L). Excludes Extra Virgin, Pomace and the rice-bran/olive blend.
Universe
117 Bengaluru pincodes — the city’s full slice of the 1,885-pincode / 25-city target universe.
Run
The one-time full per-pincode coverage pass of 29 Jun 2026 (COVERAGE_FULL=1), which scraped every Bengaluru pincode — not the daily anchor cron. Run-ids: Zepto 2026-06-29-1319, Blinkit 2026-06-29-1203, FK Minutes 2026-06-29-1605.
"Present"
Headline figures count pincodes where the SKU was scraped in stock (in_stock=1). Pincodes where it appears but is sold out are shown separately as "listed · out of stock".
Source of truth
Raw per-pincode rows in /opt/ecom-intel/data/<platform>/history.csv; serviceability cross-checked against data/coverage/ledger.csv. Physically scraped, not extrapolated.
Read me: Flipkart Minutes scrapes a login-free national catalogue, so its price is national; the serviceability per pincode (9 Bengaluru pincodes, all canola-only) is genuine.
