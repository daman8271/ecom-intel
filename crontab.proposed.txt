# and send_batch.py holds every platform's report and delivers them as ONE batch

# chain overruns, the batch goes out as soon as it's done, marked "(late by Xm)".
#
# Sizing (bootstrap data, 2026-06-05 serial sweeps): predicted total lead is
# currently ~14.8k s, clamped to LEAD_MAX-120 = 12480s (~3h28m) -> the 10:00
# sweep starts ~06:32, the 15:00 sweep ~11:32 — both inside their fire windows.
# As the chain tightens ( heal-retries, blinkit), predictions shrink and
# starts drift later automatically; the fire times below just open the window.
